Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy a course?

Select a course, sign in, choose Buy Now, follow the UPI instructions, then submit your actual UTR for administrator review.

Does submitting a UTR unlock the course instantly?

No. Your payment remains pending until the administrator independently verifies that the correct amount reached the intended account and approves it.

Where can I see payment status?

Sign in and open My Account. Payment Requests shows Pending, Approved, or Rejected status. Your dashboard also shows payment notifications.

How can I access the Telegram course group?

After approval, open My Account → My Courses → Join Telegram Group. If the group uses join-request approval, a Telegram administrator must also accept your join request.

I forgot my password. What should I do?

Visit Forgot Password. Email delivery must be configured for the platform to send a reset link. Otherwise contact support.

How do refunds or missing payments work?

See the Refund & Payment Information page and contact support with the amount, date, course and UTR. Do not share your UPI PIN or OTP.
